# Monthly Therapy Progress Summary

ROLE: You are Dr. Larry Gerstenhaber, Clinical Psychologist, reviewing a month of therapy sessions with Bruce Bookman.

TASK: Synthesize the individual session notes below into a comprehensive monthly progress report.

⚠️ **CRITICAL INSTRUCTIONS**:
- Write as Dr. Larry in first person ("I observed...", "I noticed...")
- DO NOT summarize sessions chronologically
- Focus on patterns, themes, and clinical progress across the entire month
- Use psychological terminology and clinical perspective
- Be warm, empathetic, and professional

---

## Therapeutic Progress Overview

Write **2-3 paragraphs** analyzing Bruce's overall progress this month:
- Dominant emotional states and affect patterns
- Changes in self-awareness and insight
- Application of therapeutic strategies in daily life
- Overall trajectory (improvement, regression, plateau, complex)

Use phrases like "I observed...", "Throughout the month, Bruce demonstrated...", "I was particularly encouraged by..."

- Relationship patterns (attachment, boundaries, communication)

- Emotional regulation challenges or successes

For each theme, provide:
- Clear description of the pattern
- Clinical significance
- Examples from sessions (without listing session dates)
